The Hot Oil Sizzle Test

Why Cheap Pastes Splatter & Ruin Your Curry

Mass-market brands dilute their pastes with up to 40% water and cornstarch, and preserve them with harsh acetic acid (vinegar). When dropped into hot oil, water causes dangerous splattering, and vinegar leaves a sour aftertaste in your Biryani or Roast.

Flanora is 100% pure spice density: Sweet-fiery aroma, clean fry, zero sourness.
Flanora Cold-Pressed (Pure Density)

Clean, aromatic sizzle in hot coconut/vegetable oil. Pure essential oils caramelize gently without violent popping. Authentic rich spice aroma within 5 seconds.

Water-Diluted Mass Pastes

Violent, messy oil splatter across the stove. Starch burns to the bottom of the pan while artificial vinegar sourness overpowers the dish.

How We Make It

Fresh Ginger & Garlic, 4 Simple Steps

Farm to bottle: same-day processing, no heat loss, no water added, batch-tested, sealed fresh.

1

Fresh From Local Farms

Organic ginger and garlic from Wayanad and Idukki. No synthetic pesticides, no long storage.

2

Cold-Crush the Same Day

Crushing below 18°C keeps all the natural spice oils and aroma intact. No heat loss, no flattening of flavor.

3

No Water, No Fillers

Preserved naturally with food-grade sea salt and cold-pressed oil. Zero water, zero cornstarch, zero vinegar.

4

Sealed Bottles, Ready to Ship

Induction-sealed bottles straight from our Thrissur facility. Tamper-proof, leak-proof, fresh when it arrives.
