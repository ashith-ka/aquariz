How We Work

Cold-Crush Processing, From Farm to Bottle

We handle everything in-house: sourcing fresh spices, processing the same day they arrive, testing every batch, and shipping in sealed bottles. No middlemen, no sitting around, no loss of flavor.

Cold-Crush Processing

We process in Thrissur with cold-press technology to keep natural oils intact. Induction-sealed bottles lock in freshness. GMP-compliant — we follow pharmaceutical-grade standards because fresh spice deserves it.

Batch Testing

Every batch gets tested for purity and safety. We track everything from the farm it came from to the bottle on your shelf. FSSAI compliant.

Keeping Flavor Alive

We keep improving how we crush and preserve spices. Cold-crushing keeps the natural oils intact — so your paste tastes like fresh ginger and garlic, not starch filler.

From Idea to Launch

We go from idea to finished product ourselves. That means when we make something, it's because we'd use it ourselves or know exactly why a chef or retailer needs it.

Our Story

Born from Purpose, Built to Last

"Fresh spice tastes different. We started Aquariz because store pastes taste flat, and we wanted to change that."

Why We Do This

Kerala grows incredible ginger and garlic. But most of it ends up in generic pastes that sit on shelves losing flavor. We built a processing line in Thrissur that crushes spices the same day they arrive — no heat, no fillers, no sitting around.

For home cooks who care about taste, restaurants that want consistency, and small retailers looking for products customers actually come back for.
