1. Multi-Item "WhatsApp Cart / Kitchen Bag" (Biggest Conversion Opportunity)
The Problem Now: When a user wants both Ginger-Garlic Paste (500g) and Pure Garlic Paste (200g), they have to click one product, get redirected to WhatsApp, then come back to the website to click the second product.
The Mobile Solution:
A lightweight sticky bottom "Kitchen Bag" counter (e.g., 2 Items Selected | Tap to Order on WhatsApp).
Users can tap + or - on each paste card to assemble their custom pack in 5 seconds.
Generates a single WhatsApp message listing all chosen items, quantities, and their district.
Why it works: Average order value increases significantly because customers rarely buy just one jar of paste when stocking their monthly pantry.
2. "Active Now / Quick Reply in 5 Mins" WhatsApp Status Chip
The Mobile Psychology: In India, customers often hesitate to message business WhatsApp numbers because they fear it's an unmanned account or an annoying bot that will spam them later.
The Addition:
Add a pulsing green indicator next to the WhatsApp button: 🟢 Active Now • Typical reply in under 5 minutes (with Thrissur facility hours: 8 AM – 8 PM).
Why it works: Eliminates hesitation and encourages immediate messaging while they are looking at the page.
3. Kerala District / Pincode Delivery Checker (Instant Local Assurance)
The Problem Now: Delivery terms are in a text section further down the page. Mobile users want immediate assurance: "Will this arrive at my house in Kozhikode or Aluva before Sunday?"
The Addition:
A compact one-tap dropdown or 6-digit Pincode box right under the product card:
Select: [Thrissur / Ernakulam / Palakkad] → ⚡ Free Delivery within 48 Hours
Select: [Kozhikode / Malappuram / Kannur / TVM / Other] → 🚚 Express Courier Delivery Available (COD Supported)
Why it works: Instantly answers the buyer's unspoken question before they even ask on WhatsApp.
4. Interactive "Water Dilution vs. Stone-Crushed" Comparison Slider
The Problem Now: The comparison table is great, but tables require horizontal swiping on mobile screens.
The Addition:
A visual Before/After touch toggle or visual tab:
Tab 1: Commercial Store Brand (Show photo/graphic of water separation, yellowish watery liquid, splattering oil).
Tab 2: Flanora Stone-Crushed (Show thick, coarse fibers, rich aroma, clean sizzle).
Why it works: 80% of mobile users are visual skimmers; a side-by-side visual communicates 10x faster than reading table rows.

7. Digital "1-Page Retail Margin Card" for Salesmen
The Salesman Use Case: When one of your founders or sales reps visits a local supermarket in Kochi or Thrissur, they need to share information in 10 seconds before the store manager loses patience.
The Addition:
A button on both sites: "Download 1-Page Retailer Dealership Card (Image/PDF)".
A clean, print-friendly white card showing bottle photos, retail margins (18%–25%), FSSAI, GSTIN, and order MOQ.
Can be shared instantly via WhatsApp direct share.
